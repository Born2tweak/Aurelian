# Research

Use this directory for source-backed research that informs product, domain, architecture, implementation, or verification decisions.

Research files should include:

- The question and the decision it affects.
- Sources with links or paths, dates accessed, and reliability.
- Claims separated from interpretation.
- Conflicts, stale facts, and uncertainty.
- The resulting decision, if one was made.

Do not invent facts. Do not treat low-reliability sources as project truth. Promote stable conclusions into `docs/*` or `.aurelian/decision-log.md` only when they change future decisions.
