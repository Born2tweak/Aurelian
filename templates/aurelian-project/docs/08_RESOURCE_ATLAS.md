# Resource Atlas

## Purpose

Index resources that agents and maintainers may need: docs, services, designs, dashboards, repositories, scripts, owners, and external references.

Do not invent facts. Include only resources that are known to exist, or mark them as `Unknown`.

## Required Sections

### Internal Resources

Repos, packages, docs, tickets, ADRs, design files, runbooks, and shared drives.

### External Resources

Official docs, standards, APIs, vendor pages, papers, and community references.

### Operational Resources

Dashboards, logs, alerts, deployment systems, environments, credentials process, and support channels.

### People And Ownership

Owners, reviewers, subject-matter experts, and escalation paths.

### Access Notes

Where access is required, who grants it, and what work can proceed without it.

### Resource Quality

Freshness, reliability, and whether the resource is canonical or advisory.
