# Research Gaps

## Purpose

Track missing facts whose answers could change project decisions.

Do not invent facts. A weak source does not close a gap; mark confidence honestly.

## Required Sections

### Open Questions

Question, decision affected, risk if guessed, owner/tool, and due date if any.

### Source Requirements

Preferred source quality: official docs, standards, code, tests, product evidence, user research, or expert review.

### Findings

Claim, source, date accessed, reliability, confidence, and affected decision.

### Conflicts

Where sources disagree, are stale, or do not apply to this project.

### Closed Gaps

Question, answer, evidence, confidence, and where the decision was recorded.
