# Project Bible

## Purpose

Hold durable project truths that should guide future work.

Do not invent facts. Include only evidence-backed information or clearly marked assumptions.

## Required Sections

### Product Thesis

Why does this project exist?

### Users And Jobs

Who is served, and what are they trying to accomplish?

### Domain Model

Core entities, relationships, vocabulary, and forbidden confusions.

### Non-Negotiables

Constraints that future agents must not violate.

### Operating Assumptions

Assumptions currently used for planning. Mark each as `Verified`, `Inferred`, or `Unknown`.

### Source Of Truth

Links or paths for canonical product, domain, and business facts.
