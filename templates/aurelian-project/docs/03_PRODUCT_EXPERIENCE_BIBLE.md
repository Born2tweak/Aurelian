# Product Experience Bible

## Purpose

Capture product, UX, interaction, and visual standards that should guide future user-facing work.

Do not invent facts. Base experience claims on existing product evidence, user research, screenshots, designs, or explicit decisions.

## Required Sections

### Experience Principles

Rules that describe the intended feel and behavior of the product.

### Primary Workflows

The most important user journeys and their success criteria.

### Information Hierarchy

What users should see first, second, and third on key surfaces.

### Interaction Patterns

Navigation, forms, tables, errors, empty states, loading states, confirmations, and undo behavior.

### Visual System

Typography, spacing, color roles, icon usage, density, accessibility, and responsive expectations.

### Evidence

Links to designs, screenshots, user research, analytics, or decisions.

### Unknowns

Experience questions that require research, design review, or user evidence.
