# Evidence Log

## Purpose

Track commands, inspections, reviews, screenshots, source checks, and other observations that support project claims.

Do not invent facts. Record what was observed, not what seemed likely.

## Format

### YYYY-MM-DD: Evidence Title

- Claim supported:
- Evidence type: command | test | build | lint | screenshot | source | review | inspection

- Result:
- Confidence: high | medium | low
- Limitations:
